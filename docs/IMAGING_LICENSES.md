# Imaging licenses and provenance

Every study requires a source, license, attribution, de-identification flag, educational-use flag, classification, review status, and review due date. Publication fails the database check if the essential safety fields are absent.

Do not copy internet images without documented rights. Do not upload patient images, screenshots with names, accession numbers, burned-in identifiers, or hidden metadata. A license must permit this educational web use and any derivative optimization.

Stage 5 seed visuals are original SVG illustrations generated by the application and licensed as project-owned educational visuals. They contain no patient data. Their classification is `illustrative` or `conceptual_pathology`, never `radiologic`.

Attribution must remain visible on the imaging page. Replace a visual only through a new reviewed version so links and audit history remain traceable.
